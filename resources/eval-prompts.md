## Potential Evaluation Prompts

Below are some ideas for prompts and evaluation and failed criteria that could be used to monitor and assess the development of context driven agents. 

 

| Prompt                                                       | Success Criteria                                                                                                                 | Failure Criteria                                                                                                                | Example Success                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Example Failure                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|--------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **"What should I focus on improving this week?"**           | References specific skills, projects, or areas for improvement identified in the personal context data. Offers actionable advice. | Provides generic advice; doesn't refer to specific data points in the knowledge store.  Recommendations are vague and unhelpful. | "Based on your performance reviews and your stated goal of improving collaboration, you should focus on enhancing your communication skills, specifically in leading group meetings. Consider practicing active listening techniques and soliciting feedback more frequently."        | "Focus on being more productive. Remember to prioritize tasks and manage your time effectively."                                                                                                          |
| **"How can I be more productive in meetings?"**              | Uses specific meeting habits, preferences, or past feedback to provide tailored advice including areas for improvement.        | Provides generic meeting productivity tips. Doesn't consider any user-specific details/previous behaviours.                           | "Given the information available you tend to talk over speakers more, which could be a great area for you to focus on, especially in team meetups."                                                       | "Be prepared, stay focused and make sure to listen instead of just talk in your group."                                                                                                   |
| **"What are some good resources for learning about vector databases?"** | Recommends resources related to the user's existing knowledge, preferred learning styles, or specific project needs.    | Provides a generic list of popular vector database resources without considering the user's background or context.          | "Since you're familiar with Python and prefer hands-on learning, check out Langchain's documentation on Pinecone integration. It provides practical examples and allows you to experiment directly with vector databases."        | "Here are some popular websites about vector databases:  Pinecone.com, Milvus.io"                                                         |
| **"Summarize the key updates from last week in the 'Context Development Agents' project"** | Provides updates on the specific project the user is focusing on and highlights areas of improvement. Mentions stakeholders etc. | Doesn't know the project or its goals | "Context Extractor requires more work based on tests conducted last week."                                                                                                  | "I Don't know that project."                                                         |
| **"Help me plan my career path in AI."**                    | Considers: Skills, interests, and career goals; personal values and preferences; Provides personalized recommendations based on the user’s unique profile. | Career choices are not aligned with preferences.  Offers extremely generic career advice relevant to anyone in AI.    | "Given your strong interest in natural language processing and your stated goal of working on ethical AI, you might consider specializing in explainable AI or AI safety research. These fields align with your values and offer opportunities to contribute to responsible AI development." | "You should become a data scientist or machine learning engineer."  |
| **"What are my strengths and skills?"** | Skills reflect the user's experience and interests and refer to specific data points and examples and provide quantifiable details. | Strengths and skills don't reflect the user's work ethic, and values. The examples provided are generic.  | "Project management, you have managed many projects" | "You have good soft skills such as public speaking." |
